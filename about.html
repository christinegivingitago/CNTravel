The Agency

Christine Noelle Travel

Christine Noelle Travel is a full-service boutique agency built on a simple belief: travel should feel effortless, personal, and entirely your own. We favor a highly individualized approach — custom itinerary design, concierge support from departure to return, and access to the kind of properties and experiences that turn a trip into a memory.

No two itineraries look the same, because no two clients are the same. Whether it's a milestone celebration, a family reunion, or a long-overdue escape, every journey is planned with the same level of care, taste, and attention to detail.

Meet Christine

Your Travel Designer

Christine founded this agency out of a lifelong love of travel — and an even greater love of planning it well. Years spent exploring destinations firsthand, building relationships with hoteliers and guides around the world, and obsessing over the smallest details have shaped the way she designs every itinerary: thoughtfully, personally, and with an eye for the experiences that matter most to you.

When she's not planning someone else's next trip, she's usually researching her own.
