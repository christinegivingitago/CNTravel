The Agency

Christine Noelle Travel

Christine Noelle Travel is a boutique luxury travel agency devoted to the art of traveling well.

I believe the most memorable journeys are personal—shaped by the places that inspire you, the people you share them with, and the moments you bring home. Whether it’s a long weekend away, a milestone celebration, a bucket-list adventure, or simply time to reconnect and recharge, I curate thoughtful travel experiences designed around you.

With deep roots in luxury hospitality and trusted relationships around the world, I bring intention, ease, and a personal point of view to every journey—wherever it may take you.

Meet Christine

Your Travel Designer

With more than 12 years of luxury hospitality experience, Christine brings an insider’s perspective to the world of luxury travel.

A lifelong traveler, she has explored 28 countries and lived abroad in France, experiences that have cultivated a deep appreciation for the world’s cultures, extraordinary places, and art of hospitality. She is equally passionate about food, always seeking out the restaurants, bars, and local culinary gems that make a destination memorable.

Her approach is simple: listen closely, curate thoughtfully, and create travel that feels distinctly yours—from where you stay and what you experience to where you dine along the way.
